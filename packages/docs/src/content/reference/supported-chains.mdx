# Supported Chains

Kwala Network supports 6 mainnets, 4 testnets, and the internal Kalp Chain. All chain names support fuzzy matching in tools.

## Mainnets

| Chain | Chain ID | Symbol | Aliases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ethereum | 1 | ETH | `eth`, `ethereum` |
| BNB Chain | 56 | BNB | `bnb`, `bsc` |
| Polygon | 137 | MATIC | `poly`, `polygon` |
| Avalanche C-Chain | 43114 | AVAX | `avax`, `avalanche` |
| Celo | 42220 | CELO | `celo` |
| Base | 8453 | ETH | `base` |

## Testnets

| Chain | Chain ID | Symbol | Mainnet Equivalent | Aliases |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sepolia | 11155111 | ETH | Ethereum (1) | `sepolia` |
| Polygon Amoy | 80002 | MATIC | Polygon (137) | `amoy`, `polygon amoy`, `polygon-amoy` |
| Avalanche Fuji | 43113 | AVAX | Avalanche (43114) | `fuji`, `avalanche fuji` |
| Base Sepolia | 84532 | ETH | Base (8453) | `base sepolia`, `base-sepolia`, `base_sepolia` |

## Internal

| Chain | Chain ID | Symbol | Aliases |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kalp Chain | 1905 | GINI | `kwala`, `kalp` |

The Kalp Chain (KWALA chain) is where all workflows are deployed. It is used internally for deployment transactions only.
